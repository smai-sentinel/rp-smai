# RP SMAI roadmap

Current release: **0.3.0 playable alpha**. The following are planned work, not existing features.

1. Persistent identities and saves: account/login flow, database migrations, reconnect tokens, inventories, owned cars and player apartments.
2. Driving: suspension, wheel-ground contact, better collision shapes and damage visuals. Wheel animation and gradual steering are implemented.
3. Characters: multiple bodies, faces and outfits, aim/reload/enter-car animation clips, animation blending and richer full-body poses. Basic arm IK is implemented.
4. City art: individually authored streets and buildings, more interiors, signage, stores, terrain elevation, traffic signals and bridges. The current city is generated, with one bank interior and a simple jail view.
5. Roleplay: police dispatch, evidence and citations, EMS roles, businesses, economy balancing, more jobs and cooperative missions.
6. AI: lane-based routing and traffic rules, pedestrians avoiding cars, coordinated pursuit, officers leaving vehicles and better obstacle navigation.
7. Multiplayer: persistence, lag compensation, client prediction/reconciliation, bandwidth profiling and load tests before raising the 16-player cap.
8. Voice: two-device acceptance testing, hosted TURN with temporary credentials, local talk indicators and optional radio channels.
9. Content: original vehicles and approved licenses for any actual manufacturer models, additional weapon types, authored audio and music.
10. Distribution: monitoring, backups, accessibility pass and controller support. Public hosting and touch controls are implemented.

GTA-scale content, photoreal faces, cinematics, licensed vehicles and a vast handcrafted map require a larger art, animation, design and engineering production. They are not represented as complete in this release.
